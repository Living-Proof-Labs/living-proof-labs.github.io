# Roadmap

What works today, and what is still integration work. Listing an item under
"Building" does not promise a date.

## Available now

- Versioned capability specifications.
- Deterministic fixtures and recorded or scripted (mock) assessments.
- Explicit, versioned grading policy and portable reports.
- A standalone MuJoCo example that exercises a native simulation engine.

[Try the quickstart](quickstart.md) or
[download a released example](vision.html#see-it-today).

## Building

- Connected simulation verification: running capability scenarios in a simulator
  and feeding the evidence into evaluation.
- Physical-robot execution, in its own controlled hardware environment.
- Live evaluator providers.
- Automated diagnosis-to-code workflows.

Reports do not certify safety, and an engine demonstration is not evidence of an
integrated pipeline.
